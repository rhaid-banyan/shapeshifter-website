Company

We turn geometry into margin.

Shapeshifter exists because the apparel industry’s biggest cost — fabric — was being planned with estimates instead of mathematics. We’ve spent two decades fixing that, one cutting room at a time.

Our Story

A head for mathematics. A heart for business.

In 1999, Dr. Hamish Dean was deep in a PhD with an unglamorous obsession: how to pack container drums so that nothing — not a centimetre — was wasted. When word of that work reached Macpac, the New Zealand outdoor brand recognised the same problem in a very different material. The auto-nesting code Hamish wrote for Macpac’s fabric became the technical foundation of Shapeshifter — and the start of a twenty-year mission to take the guesswork out of how the world’s garments get made.

Today the same conviction drives everything we build: don’t just solve the problem — make sure you’re solving the right problem.
