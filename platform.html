The Platform

Meet Prophet.

Prophet is Shapeshifter’s optimization platform. It computes markers and cut plans for hundreds of scenarios simultaneously — all the options, all the scenarios, all the time — so the plan you cut is the best one that exists, not the first one that worked.

How Prophet Works

From upload to cutting floor. The Infinity Engine.

1
Upload

Upload the PO

Size ratios, quantities, CAD files and fabric constraints — uploaded once. That’s the whole job.

2
Compute

Every option, computed

Prophet computes hundreds of marker and cut-plan scenarios simultaneously, for each order — the Marker Multiverse.

3
Choose

Choose what wins

Direct Prophet to what matters today — lowest cost, least fabric, or fastest — and the chosen plan flows to production. Something changes? Prophet recomputes.

Traditional systems make one cut plan. Prophet makes them all.

A planner produces the single plan there’s time for. Prophet’s cloud computes all the scenarios for every style — eliminating the uncertainty in buying and cutting. That’s the difference between digitizing a manual process and eliminating it.
