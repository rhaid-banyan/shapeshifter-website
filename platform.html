The Platform

Meet Prophet.

Prophet is Shapeshifter’s optimization platform. It computes markers and cut plans for hundreds of styles simultaneously — all the options, all the styles, all the time — so the plan you cut is the best one that exists, not the first one that worked.

How Prophet Works

From upload to cutting floor.

01 · UPLOAD

PO + CAD in

Size ratios, quantities and pattern files — uploaded once.

02 · COMPUTE

Every marker, every plan

Prophet computes thousands of marker and cut-plan combinations simultaneously, powered by its Infinity Engine.

03 · COMPARE

Ranked by what matters

Prophet ranks every plan for lowest fabric use, fastest cutting, or your custom balance of both.

04 · CUT

To the floor

The chosen plan flows to production. If the PO changes, Prophet recomputes instantly.

Less effort. No guesswork.

Traditional systems make one cut plan. Prophet makes them all.

A CAD operator produces the single plan there’s time for. Prophet’s cloud computes the full space of options for every style — eliminating manual estimation and uncertainty in costing and cutting. That’s the difference between digitizing a manual process and eliminating it.
