Sustainability

The greenest fabric is the fabric you never cut.

Every percentage point of fabric saved is waste that never enters the supply chain — savings the industry can measure in yards, water and emissions. Shapeshifter customers reduce fabric waste at the earliest step possible — buying and cutting less — helping them meet the sustainability targets their brands are setting.
