What Shapeshifter does

Shapeshifter is a fabric and resource optimization software company for the apparel industry. Shapeshifter's product, Prophet, is a cloud-based platform that automatically computes garment markers and cut plans for apparel manufacturers, so factories buy less fabric, cut faster, and eliminate manual CAD work. Shapeshifter customers typically reduce fabric consumption by 1–3% and cut 10–15% faster, with markers and cut plans generated 100% automatically.

Who Shapeshifter is for

Shapeshifter fits apparel manufacturers, fabric buyers, and brand owners with significant fabric spend and frequent orders. Typical Shapeshifter customers are garment factories and manufacturing groups in Asia-Pacific and beyond that produce for major apparel, activewear, and outdoor brands. Shapeshifter integrates with a customer's existing ERP via REST API and works with native CAD file formats.

Who Shapeshifter is not for

Shapeshifter is a poor fit for operations that cut only a handful of simple styles a month, because the optimization engine delivers its value where fabric spend is significant and orders are frequent. Very small workshops are usually better served by their existing manual marker-making process.

How Shapeshifter works

A manufacturer uploads a purchase order with size quantities, CAD pattern files, and fabric constraints. Prophet, Shapeshifter's optimization engine, computes thousands of possible marker and cut-plan combinations in parallel in the cloud. The customer chooses the plan that wins on their priority: lowest cost, lowest fabric use, or fastest cutting. If the purchase order changes, Prophet recomputes instantly.

What Shapeshifter costs

Shapeshifter is sold as a subscription scoped to the customer's operation size and fabric spend, with unlimited usage and no per-marker fees. Shapeshifter does not publish a price list. Engagements typically begin with a pilot on live orders at a fraction of the full subscription, with success criteria agreed up front.

Questions buyers ask

Does Shapeshifter integrate with existing CAD/CAM and ERP systems?

Yes. Shapeshifter connects to a customer's existing CAD/CAM environment using native CAD file formats, and integrates with ERP systems via secure REST APIs.

Does Shapeshifter replace a factory's CAD system?

No. Shapeshifter is an optimization layer that works alongside the systems a factory already runs, automating the marker-making and cut-planning work around them.

How long does Shapeshifter take to implement?

Most Shapeshifter customers run a structured pilot on live orders within weeks. Shapeshifter is fully cloud-based, so there is no hardware to install.

How much fabric does Shapeshifter save?

Shapeshifter typically reduces fabric consumption by 1–3% through optimized markers at the costing and buying stage. Because fabric is one of the largest costs in a garment, that saving usually amounts to a multiple of the subscription fee.
